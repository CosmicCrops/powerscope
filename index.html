Ticket to Work

The Ticket to Work Program

Ticket to Work is Social Security's free, voluntary program for people ages 18–64 who receive SSDI or SSI and want to work. It connects you with an Employment Network like Prestige Path Consulting for services and support — at no cost to you. Prestige Path's Ticket to Work tools include an SSI worksheet modeled on Cornell University's Yang-Tan Institute, so you can see how earnings and benefits fit together.

If you would like to enroll, or learn more about the Ticket to Work Program, please visit www.prestigepathconsulting.com or send an email to Prestigepath@consultant.com.

Two numbers worth understanding

Two ideas come up again and again when you work while on benefits. You don't have to master them alone — but knowing what they are makes the first conversation easier.

Trial Work Period (TWP)

The Trial Work Period lets SSDI beneficiaries test working while keeping their benefits. The TWP does not apply to SSI.

Substantial Gainful Activity (SGA)

SGA is the earnings level SSA uses in its disability decisions — the line earnings are measured against once trial work is done.

Savings

ABLE Accounts

Learn how an ABLE account lets eligible people with disabilities save money — including for work-related goals — without the savings counting against SSI and Medicaid resource limits the way a regular account would.

Work Goal Plan

P.A.S.S. — Plan to Achieve Self-Support

See how a PASS plan can let you set aside income for a work goal — training, equipment, or other items you need for a job — while SSA excludes that income when figuring your SSI.
